Pre-construction model and plan check

For builders and remodelers. Find the drawing mistakes before framing starts.

What it is

Before framing starts, I build the job in 3D from the plan set, to the inch, and check every sheet against the others. You get a list of every place the drawings disagree, with the sheets involved and what the model used. You also get a walkthrough you can share with your client.

What you get

  • A numbered list of drawing conflicts, each with the sheets and the two readings
  • 3D views of each problem spot
  • Elevations and sections cut from the model
  • A walkthrough link that opens on a computer or a phone
  • The 3D model files, if you want them

How it works

  1. 1
    Send the PDF plan set.
  2. 2
    I build the model from the written dimensions.
  3. 3
    I check the plans, elevations, sections and details against each other and against the printed numbers.
  4. 4
    You get the list and the walkthrough. Send them to the architect before anything is ordered or cut.

See a sample

On a three story shore house, the check found three things. A stair has 72.7 in of headroom where the plans call for 80 in. A sliding door is drawn 5 1/2 in narrower on the elevation than on the plan. A floor level is printed two different ways.
